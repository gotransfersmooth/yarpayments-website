GOOD QUESTIONS

A little more clarity.

Is Yara Pay available to use today?

Yara Pay is in development, with a working prototype and partner discussions underway. Public availability depends on regulatory approvals and partner integrations. Contact us to learn more.

Is Yara Pay a bank?

No. Yara Pay is being built as an Open Finance technology and distribution platform. Banking, remittance and insurance products will be supplied by the relevant regulated partners.

Who is Yara Pay being built for?

Our focus is UAE’s people and small retail businesses who find traditional financial services difficult to access, along with the institutions and employers serving them.

How can my organisation get involved?

We welcome conversations with banks, exchange houses, insurers, bill payment providers, employers and platforms. Tell us about your organisation through the contact form. Let’s start a conversation.
