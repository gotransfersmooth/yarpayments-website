GOOD QUESTIONS

A little more clarity.

Is Yara Pay available to use today?

Not yet. We have a working prototype. Launch depends on approvals and partner integrations.

Is Yara Pay a bank?

No. We’re an Open Finance platform. Regulated partners will provide the financial products.

Who is Yara Pay being built for?

UAE’s people and small businesses who need easier access to everyday finance.

How can my organisation get involved?

Banks, exchange houses, insurers, payment providers, employers and platforms: introduce yourself through our contact form.
